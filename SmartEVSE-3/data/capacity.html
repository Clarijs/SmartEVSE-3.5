Capacity mode

Enable only if your electricity provider has a capacity (peak kW) rate: exceeding a set power in a billing period costs more. Requires a Mains meter that feeds kWh data to the SmartEVSE.

Peak: limits the mains power to the monthly peak minus the margin, recalculated with every meter update using the measured voltage. The monthly peak starts at 2500 W each month, is raised automatically when a 15-minute average breaks it, and can be synced from your digital meter via MQTT /Set/CapacityPeak. Send the voltage as 6th value of /Set/MainsMeter (L1:L2:L3:W:Wh:V, V in 0.1 V); otherwise 230 V is assumed.
